# JurisLink — Project Context & Blueprint

## Project Identity
- **Name:** JurisLink
- **Domain:** Global Cybercrime Jurisprudence, Statutory Strictness Matrix & Live Threat Intelligence
- **Context:** Semester 7 Cybercrime & Cyber Law Mini Project
- **Target Audience:** Academic evaluators, university professors, legal researchers, cybersecurity analysts
- **Deployment Target:** Vercel (Production zero-config serverless deployment)

## Core Value Proposition
JurisLink is a high-end, tactical cyber legal intelligence platform that bridges the gap between active cyber threat vectors (cross-border attacks, ransomware syndicates, state-sponsored APTs) and the global statutory frameworks that govern them. It enables:
1. **Interactive 2D Scrollable Tactical Plane Map:** Pan-and-zoom global map rendering real-time animated curved attack arcs between nation-states, with a toggleable Legal Strictness Heatmap mode.
2. **Side-by-Side Jurisdictional Strictness Comparison:** Deep comparative analysis of statutory incarceration terms, turnover penalties, and mandatory incident reporting windows.
3. **Pending & Unnotified Acts Pipeline:** Explicit tracking of statutes passed by legislatures but awaiting official gazette notification or secondary procedural rules.
4. **AI Cybercrime Matrix:** Dedicated legal taxonomy for deepfakes, voice cloning extortion, autonomous malware agents, and model training poisoning.
5. **Real-Time AI Legal Researcher:** Grounded intelligence powered by Groq Llama-3.3-70B and Tavily Web Search API.

## Design Philosophy: High-End Cyber Intelligence (Anti-"AI Slop")
- **Inspirations:** Palantir Gotham, CrowdStrike Falcon OverWatch, Cloudflare Radar, Recorded Future.
- **Palette:** Deep Obsidian (`#030712`), Slate Surface (`#0b0f19`), Technical Borders (`#1e293b`), Tactical Cyan (`#00f0ff`), Crimson Alert (`#f43f5e`), Warning Amber (`#f59e0b`).
- **Typography:** Technical monospace data headers (`JetBrains Mono`/`Geist Mono`), clean modern sans typography for legal prose.
- **HUD Micro-Interactions:** Corner brackets, live telemetry badges, tactical status chips, smooth Bezier arc rendering.
